02 · Connections & Power-Up

Connections

  • INPUT L / INPUT R — instrument or stereo source in.
  • OUTPUT L / OUTPUT R — to your amp, interface, or next pedal.
  • 9V DC — power. Match the polarity symbol printed on the pedal.
  • microSD card — insert before powering on. The pedal only looks for the card at start-up.

Your live signal always passes through the pedal. The loop is mixed on top of it.

Power-up

  1. A colour sweep runs across the LEDs while the display spins.
  2. The LEDs fade in and out once: red = Manual record mode, blue = Auto record mode.
  3. The pedal is ready. LEDs off, display shows the slot number, no loop loaded.
Important

The loop you are playing is not kept when power is removed. Save it to a memory slot first.

All LEDs solid red with 00 for two seconds at start-up means the card could not be read. You can still loop, but you cannot save or load.

03 · Quick Start
  1. Tap REC/DUB. LEDs sweep red. Play.
  2. Tap REC/DUB again. The loop plays. A green bar shows your place.
  3. Tap REC/DUB again to overdub. Orange flash. Tap once more to finish.
  4. Tap STOP to stop. Tap REC/DUB to play from the top.
  5. To clear: double-tap STOP and hold the second tap for 1.5 seconds. Two red blinks.

While recording

  • The display counts seconds. At each minute, the minute number blinks.
  • In the last 15 seconds before the length limit, it counts down 15 → 00.
  • At the limit, the loop closes itself and starts playing.

Overdubbing

  • New playing is added on top. Nothing is erased.
  • The bar turns orange over the part you have covered. Green is not yet covered.
  • You can overdub with SPEED or DIRECTION flipped. For the cleanest result, overdub at the speed the loop was recorded at.

Clearing the loop

  1. Tap STOP.
  2. Within half a second, press STOP again and hold for 1.5 seconds. LEDs go solid red.
  3. Two quick red blinks = cleared. Let go early to cancel.

Record order — Mode A / Mode B

  • Mode A: Record → Play → Overdub.
  • Mode B: Record → Overdub → Play. The second REC tap goes straight into overdub.
  • To switch: hold REC/DUB + STOP together for 2 seconds while empty, playing, or stopped. Press STOP a hair first.
  • The LEDs show the new order, left to right: record, play, overdub. They flash twice to confirm.
  • This choice is remembered on the card.

Auto record

  • Turn on in the menu (A2).
  • Tap REC/DUB to arm. LEDs breathe blue.
  • Recording starts the moment you play.
  • Tap REC/DUB again, or STOP, to disarm.
05 · Loop Length

The EFFECT toggle position at the moment you start recording sets the maximum length.

EFFECT toggle at record startStereoMonoUndo
UNDO1:303:00Yes
ONE3:006:00Only if the loop ends up 1:30 or shorter (3:00 mono)
  • Stereo or mono is chosen in the menu (A1).
  • Mono blends left and right into one channel and doubles the recording time.
06 · Undo / Redo
  • Set the EFFECT toggle to UNDO.
  • Tap EFFECT: the last overdub is removed. Blue flash.
  • Tap again: it comes back. Green flash.
  • One level. One step = one overdub, from REC in to REC out.
  • The first recording cannot be undone.
  • Works while playing or stopped.
Good to know
  • Undo is not available on loops longer than 1:30 stereo / 3:00 mono. You get a red flash.
  • If you undo a layer and then start a new overdub, the undone layer comes back for good.
  • If you save while a layer is undone, the saved loop still includes it.
  • Loading a slot clears the undo history.
07 · One-Shot
  • Set the EFFECT toggle to ONE.
  • Tap EFFECT: the loop jumps to the start, plays once, and stops. Purple flash, purple bar.
  • With DIRECTION on REV, it starts from the end and plays backward.
  • Tap again any time to re-trigger. Good for stabs and samples.
  • Tap REC/DUB during a one-shot to cancel it. The loop keeps looping.
08 · Speed & Direction

Speed

Works like tape. The SPEED position when you start recording is "normal" for that loop.

Recorded atFlip toYou hear
FULLHALFHalf speed, one octave down
HALFFULLDouble speed, one octave up
  • The change is instant.
  • A loaded memory slot treats the SPEED position at load time as normal.
  • SPEED is ignored while time-stretch is on.

Direction

  • Flip to REV to play the loop backward. Instant.
  • The progress bar runs the other way.
09 · Time-Stretch

Changes the tempo without changing the pitch.

  1. While a loop is playing forward, press and hold the BANK encoder.
  2. Start turning within 2 seconds. (If you wait, the menu opens instead.)
  3. Clockwise = faster. Counter-clockwise = slower. Nine steps each way, about 8% per step.
  4. Let go. The new tempo stays.
  • Display: 1– 9 faster, -1–-9 slower, 00 normal.
  • Full range: half tempo to double tempo.
  • To turn it off: hold the encoder, turn back to 00, let go.
  • A new recording, clearing, or loading a slot also resets it.
  • Not available in reverse (red flash) or on stream slots.

Bake — make the new tempo permanent

  • With stretch on, tap REC/DUB.
  • The pedal rewrites the loop at the new tempo and goes straight into overdub. No gap in the sound.
  • What you play during the bake is kept.
  • The new loop takes over at the next loop start. Stretch returns to 00.
  • REC taps are ignored until that happens.
Good to know
  • A bake cannot be undone. Save to a slot first if you want the original.
  • Bake needs the loop — before and after — to fit in 1:30 stereo / 3:00 mono. If not, REC just starts a normal overdub at the original tempo.
10 · Level & Crush

Level

  • Loop volume, silent to full. Your live signal is not affected.
  • While turning: yellow bar, display shows 0–100 (00 = 100).

Crush

  • Lo-fi on the loop only: narrowed tone, lowered sample rate and bit depth, compression, soft saturation, and a slow tape warble.
  • Fully clockwise = clean (true bypass). Counter-clockwise = more crush.
  • While turning: white bar. The display follows the knob, so 00 (100) is clean and 0 is fully crushed.
  • Three strengths — Light, Normal, Heavy — in the menu (A3).
Note

Level and Crush are playback only. They are never recorded into overdubs or saved to slots. To print Crush into a file, export from the web app.

11 · Memory Slots

99 slots on the microSD card. Turn BANK to choose one. Every encoder click gives a short blue flash.

Load a slot

  1. Turn BANK to the slot.
  2. Double-click the encoder. The number blinks.
  3. Click once more to confirm. (STOP cancels. It also gives up after 10 seconds.)
  4. Blue bar fills, then a green flash. The loop is loaded and stopped.
  5. Tap REC/DUB to play.

Red flash = the slot is empty or could not be read.

Save to a slot

  1. Turn BANK to the slot.
  2. Click once, then press again and hold for 3 seconds.
  3. While holding, the LEDs breathe yellow (empty slot) or red (you will overwrite). Let go early to cancel.
  4. A bar fills, then two flashes. Saved. The loop is stopped.
  • You cannot save or load while recording or overdubbing.
  • The pedal does not show which slots are full. The save colour (yellow/red) tells you.
  • Slots are deleted in the web app, not on the pedal.
  • After a save, wait a moment before removing power.

Stream slots

Long tracks (backing tracks, full songs) loaded with the web app play straight from the card.

  • Any length. Load almost instantly.
  • Play-only: no overdub, no undo, no save, no time-stretch.
  • REC/DUB = play. STOP = stop and rewind.
  • EFFECT toggle on UNDO: the EFFECT footswitch is pause / resume.
  • SPEED, DIRECTION, and one-shot work. Expect a brief gap when flipping direction or re-triggering.
12 · Menu

Every setting is one move: hold a footswitch, move a control. The display and LEDs show the result right away.

Getting in and out

Settings

Tips
  • Holding REC/DUB alone shows the A1 value. Read a knob setting while the knob is still moving.
  • The knobs are live. Re-set LEVEL and CRUSH after you leave the menu.
  • Settings are stored on the card when you exit. A red flash on exit means they were not stored.

Factory reset

  1. Hold REC/DUB + STOP while powering on.
  2. The display counts 05 → 01. Keep holding to the end.
  3. Menu settings return to defaults. Let go early to cancel.

Memory slots and the Mode A/B choice are not touched.

13 · FX2 Mode

Turns the STOP footswitch into a second effect switch. Turn on in the menu (C3).

STOP footswitch in FX2 mode

  • Single tap: its effect. (Default: reverse.)
  • Double-tap: stop.
  • Double-tap and hold 1.5 s: clear the loop.

Effects you can assign

EffectWhat a tap does
ClassicFollows the EFFECT toggle (one-shot or undo)
Speed latchFlips speed. Tap again to release.
Reverse latchFlips direction. Tap again to release.
Undo / redoSame as Undo
One-shotSame as One-shot
  • While a latch is on, the matching toggle is ignored.
  • Latches reset on a new loop, a clear, or a slot load.

Assigning

  1. Enter the menu, with C3 set to on.
  2. Hold EFFECT or STOP — the footswitch you want to set.
  3. Flip a toggle: SPEED = speed latch. DIRECTION = reverse latch. EFFECT = classic.

If both footswitches are set to classic, the pedal splits them: the one you just set becomes one-shot, the other becomes undo/redo.

16 · The microSD Card
  • Format: FAT32. Name the card CYCLES (the web app checks the name).
  • Insert before power-on. Swapping cards while running is not supported — power off first.
  • The pedal creates its folders on first start.
On the cardWhat it is

Cycles/Memory_Slots/Mem_NN_mono.rawMono loop
Cycles/Memory_Slots/Mem_NN_stream.rawStereo stream (play-only, any length)

Audio is stored as raw 24-bit, 48 kHz. Use the web app to get loops in and out — don't rename files by hand. Don't use slot 00.

17 · Data Manager Web App

cycleslooper.com manages the card from your computer. Use desktop Chrome or Edge. Phones can only preview the stock tracks.

Connect

  • Put the card in your computer. Click Connect CYCLES Card and choose the card.
  • No card handy? Click Look around without a card.

Import

  • Drop in WAV, MP3, or other audio. It is converted to 48 kHz and levelled automatically.
  • WAV is best for loops. MP3 adds tiny gaps that can throw off loop timing.
  • Up to 1:30 goes in as a normal stereo loop. Longer files ask you to choose a format:
FormatMax lengthOverdubUndo
Stereo1:30YesYes
Stereo long3:00YesNo
Mono3:00YesYes
Mono long6:00YesNo
Stereo streamAnyNoNo
Important

For long tracks choose stereo stream. Firmware 1.6.6 cannot play mono stream files — the slot will look empty on the pedal.

Memory slots

  • Drag a tile to move it to another slot. Dropping on a full slot asks first.
  • Hover a tile and click × to delete. Delete All Slots wipes everything. Both ask first.

Stock tracks

  • Backing tracks you can preview and send to a slot. Click to import, or drag onto a slot.

Preview and export

  • Audition any slot with speed (0.5× / 1× / 2×), direction, Crush, loop, and one-shot. Space bar = play / pause.
  • Export saves a WAV with your speed and direction applied.
  • Apply Effects = Yes also prints Crush into the file.
  • Preview settings never change what is on the card.
